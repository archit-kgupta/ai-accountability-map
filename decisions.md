## Borderline calls kept, with reasons
- AIFS0039 Pathlight, rules No: says the challenges "necessitate new regulations, or at least guidance"; the first preference is new regulation.
- AIFS0063 PUFin, rules Partly: proposes specific new duties on providers within the FCA framework.
- AIFS0078 Bangor, rules Partly: wants clear accountability frameworks maintained and FCA data on AI breaches; data protection proposals are other law.
- AIFS0013 Krupiy, rules Not stated: main ask is revising the Equality Act 2010 (other law).
- AIFS0044 City of London Corporation and AIFS0081 APPG: actor_type Other, since neither is a regulator or government department.
- AIFS0080 CII and AIFS0087 IFoA: professional bodies coded Industry body.
- AIFS0076 Sutton & Williams: Think tank, written with the Centre for the Governance of AI.
